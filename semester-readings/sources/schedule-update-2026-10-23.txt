Important topics we probably won't get to cover in-depth are:

1) theories about why people (and firms and countries) pursue or adopt certain technologies over alternatives when multiple solutions exist.
2) how countries slow themselves down: patterns in organizational inertia, bureaucratic in-fighting, and social resistance to certain technologies.
3) confusion about which technologies to prioritize: technology hype cycles, offset cycles.
4) measuring technology competition: what does it mean for a country to "be ahead", and how can we know?


All of these are still super important to the geopolitics of technological innovation, so if you are interested in these questions please consider the relevant parts of the syllabus as suggested readings. I am also happy to discuss anytime!
