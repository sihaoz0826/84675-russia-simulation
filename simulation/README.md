## Strategy in one paragraph

Russia has the lowest wealth but +7 tacit knowledge and "late-game disruptive potential." The plan is to build, then break out: earn early points with low-risk (L) applied research, buy basic research to reach the medium/high (M/H) thresholds while free-riding on rivals' public basic research, invest in education early, keep immigration closed, start espionage against the US and France (the rules' espionage-vulnerable countries) from Round 2-3, open a bounded H position by Round 3, and keep a 20% reserve. Round 1: 45% L, 20% basic research, 15% education, 0% espionage, 20% saved.
