## Final report outline to complete after play

The syllabus calls for a **2-3 page team report**, with labor divided equally. Due December 2 per the instructor's final syllabus update. The outline below maps directly to syllabus p. 4.

1. **How gameplay unfolded at each stage.** Explain the actual opening and subsequent stages, using the timeline. Record decisive outcomes and distinguish intended effects from luck. Do not retell every dice roll if it did not affect the argument.
2. **Beliefs about relative progress.** Describe what the team thought about Russia's position at each stage, what information supported that belief, and how confidence changed. Separate what was known then from what became visible at the end.
3. **Evolution from Round 1.** Explain whether, how, and why each major recommendation changed or stayed stable. Cite actual internal disagreement and the rule used to decide.
4. **Why the team won or what it could have done differently.** Use the official outcome. If Russia won, discuss its decisions and plausible rival missteps without inventing motives. If it did not, identify feasible alternative decisions at the time and explain how they could have changed the outcome. Distinguish expected improvement from a guaranteed win.
5. **Optional mechanics reflection.** Discuss model simplifications such as instant espionage copying, binary immigration/security, or the relation between TPs and wider diffusion. Suggest a specific improvement and its tradeoff.

Aim for an integrated explanation, not separate portfolio mini-essays. Allocate approximately half a page to the opening and initial beliefs, one page to changes across actual stages, and half to one page to the outcome and counterfactual. Adjust to the required 2-3 pages and actual evidence.
